# Day-1 Of #90DaysOfDevOps

## My understanding of DevOps 
DevOps is a culture and set of practices that brings development and operations teams together to automate and improve the software delivery lifecycle. It uses practices like CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, automation, monitoring, and continuous feedback to deliver software faster, reliably, and consistently.

## WHhy am i learning DevOps?
I’m learning DevOps because I want to move beyond traditional IT support and system administration into a more automation- and cloud-focused role. With my experience in Windows administration and troubleshooting, I already have a good understanding of servers, operating systems, incidents, and infrastructure.

DevOps gives me the opportunity to build on that foundation by learning technologies like Linux, Git, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, cloud platforms, and monitoring.

I’m particularly interested in automation because it can reduce repetitive manual tasks, improve reliability, and make deployments faster and more consistent. My goal is to use my existing infrastructure experience along with DevOps skills to grow into a DevOps or SRE-oriented role.
